Our Heritage & Story

Serving Kozhikode with Love & Style

Colour Plus Since 1998

Colour Plus is a trusted family clothing destination located on the legendary S.M. Street (Mittai Theruvu), Kozhikode, happily dressing generations since 1998.

Popularly known among Calicut shoppers as "The Ocean of Garments", we take pride in presenting an extensive collection of clothing for growing boys, cheerful girls, and modern women looking for elegant everyday kurtis, festive party gowns, and special occasion dresses.

For over 25+ golden years, our ethos has remained simple: providing the friendliest shopping experience, genuine fabric quality, and honest, affordable prices in the warmest festive spirit of Kozhikode.
